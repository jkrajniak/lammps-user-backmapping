# Add a core guard on attracting 1-4 pairs without LJ (`simulation.core_guard`)

## Why

The robust protocol crashes the PET/Dacron example at 4 MPI ranks, about 2000 steps after the
force cap is released (`Bond atoms ... missing`, the same step and bond in three identical runs).
The 8-rank run finishes, but by chance. Both have the same defect
(`research/experiments/20261027_ring-guard-prototype.md`):

- OPLS-AA gives the acid hydrogen (opls_270) no LJ, so the 1-4 pair of the acid H and its own
  carbonyl O has only the scaled Coulomb attraction (fudgeQQ 0.5). The published TER topology also
  makes the O=C-OH angle soft (200 kJ/mol/rad^2, about a third of OPLS).
- During the hot early stages (lambda = 0 relaxation, start of the ramp) the acid H falls onto
  the carbonyl O: 0.006 A, O-C-O 45 deg, -4000 to -7700 kcal/mol of 1-4 Coulomb per pair, in 91
  to 100 of the 243 COOH groups. The capped forces and nve/limit hold the collapsed pairs through
  the ramp.
- At the switch to the staged NVT the pairs come apart. The 1-4 Coulomb energy rises by about
  100000 kcal/mol within 1000 steps and goes into the acid hydrogens (about 1300 K for the type
  against 300 K for the rest). After the cap release the hottest one hits a ring H and the run
  crashes. The collapse is not a placement error: at step 0 every COOH is intact (2.22 A).

## What changes

- `simulation.core_guard` (robust protocol, on by default, `core_guard: null` switches it off):
  every 1-4 pair with a Coulomb scale above 0, opposite charges, and no LJ term (sigma or epsilon
  0) gets an LJ core 4 eps ((sigma/r)^12 - (sigma/r)^6) through a second `fix backmap/pairs`
  (LJ only, `at` weighting), from the first minimization until the hybrid structure is written,
  released together with the ring guard and the stereo restraint. The production force field is
  unchanged.
  - `simulation.core_guard`: sigma in nm (default 0.15).
  - `simulation.core_guard_epsilon`: epsilon in kJ/mol (default 0.2092, 0.05 kcal/mol). At the
    normal 1-4 distance of the COOH pair (0.22 nm) the term is -0.02 kcal/mol, at 0.10 nm +24,
    at 0.08 nm +370.
  - Files `core_guard.dat`, `core_guard.lmp`, `core_guard_release.lmp`.
- No new LAMMPS style.

## Validation

1. Unit tests: pair selection (attracting, no LJ, Coulomb on), files and units, defaults, the
   robust protocol includes the guard before the minimization and releases it after the hybrid
   structure is written and before production.
2. PET, 4 ranks, the input that crashed, plus the guard on the 243 carbonyl O / acid H pairs
   only: no pair below 1 A from step 11000 on, no jump of the 1-4 Coulomb energy, acid O-H
   fluctuation temperature 292-317 K, the run finishes.
3. PET, 4 ranks, input generated by this branch with the defaults (659 pairs: also acid H /
   negative ring C and hydroxyl H / negative aliphatic H): same criteria.

## Out of scope

- Changing the published TER angle constant or giving polar hydrogens an LJ core in the
  production force field.
- The bakery protocol.
